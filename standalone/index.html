Safety & Rules

No. Hammer Boarding House is a female-only boarding house. No boys are allowed. This is one of the house rules set by the landlord.

Yes, a curfew applies at Hammer Boarding House. The current curfew time is confirmed by management when you book. Message +260 977 802 016 on WhatsApp and we will tell you the times for this term.

Visitors are limited to female friends and close relatives. Male visitors are not permitted. Speak to management about visiting arrangements before anyone comes to stay.

Security is provided at Hammer Boarding House and is included in your rent. The landlord describes the surrounding Silverest area as very secure. If you would like to see the setup for yourself, you are welcome to arrange a viewing before you commit.
